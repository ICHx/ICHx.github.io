Extension Appreciation: Andrew's FastStream

Was looking into Apple's "Liquid Glass showcase: Slack" case-study, but the video buffering was incredibly slow and no fast seek support.

This extension works as a in-place video player replacement.  With it the stream went from a simple built-in video player to a full-featured player with multithreaded streaming, fast-seek with thumbnail, so on.

Supported for Firefox and Chrome

https://github.com/Andrews54757/FastStream
