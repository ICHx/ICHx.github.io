Introduce to you `lnav`, a log viewer in your terminal.
I was looking for something to colourize million lines of log without needing to open vscode, and it came in handy.

If you are already familiar with `less` or `k9s`, you are good to go already.

A few useful keys:
/ - quick search
n - next match
p - previous match
q - exit

https://github.com/tstack/lnav
